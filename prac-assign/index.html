About ABES Engineering College

Building Future Engineers

ABES Engineering College is one of the premier engineering institutions in Uttar Pradesh, committed to academic excellence, research, innovation, entrepreneurship and holistic development.

With highly qualified faculty members, modern laboratories, digital classrooms, industry collaborations and excellent placement opportunities, ABES prepares students for successful careers in technology and engineering.

Our mission is to inspire creativity, leadership and lifelong learning while nurturing responsible professionals who can contribute to society.
